Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Spaza Books collects ledger images uploaded by users solely for the purpose of extracting financial data (income, expenses, and credit records) via AI OCR processing. We also store transaction values in secure databases to maintain your financial records.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide, maintain, and improve our financial tracking features for informal traders. We do not sell or share your business data with third parties.

3. Data Security

We leverage industry-standard security protocols, including Supabase encryption and Row Level Security (RLS), to ensure your financial logs remain private and protected.
